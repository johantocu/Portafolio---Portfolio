Chapter · The challenge and the idea

A loved product, a scattered brand.

5.0★Google · 27 reviews

  1. 01Two social accounts and about 540 followers
  2. 02Different prices in store and on Wolt
  3. 03An old "nowhere to sit" story

The problem was reach and consistency, not quality.
The idea
A hot dog is a small, perfect machine.

Bun, sausage, heat, crunch and sauce. Frank's doesn't pretend to be anything else: it just gets every part right. The brand dresses that honesty in a sunny, cheeky world.

Groovy cartoon + American diner + Maltese summer
= Mediterranean Retro Street Diner
02
Chapter · Brand strategy

Malta's street hot dog, with character.

PurposeTo be Malta's favourite hot dog at any hour: by day, at the beach or in the small hours.
PositioningNeither pretentious gourmet nor soulless fast food. Tasty, fun and made to share.
AudienceLocals aged 18 to 35, expats in Sliema, Gżira and Msida, summer tourists and late-night cravings.
ValueHonest hot dogs, an addictive sauce and a brand you want to photograph.
Tasty honesty Attitude Closeness Consistency Endless summer

Chapter · Digital experience

Ordering, on screen and in store.

Three interfaces on the same system: the delivery app, the in-store menu and the interactive screen that rotates the combos. They're live: you can tap them.

User experience Designed around a single journey.

A brand that speaks the same way in store, on delivery and on social: strategy, Frankie, a design system and three ready interfaces.

The product was already good; what was missing was a brand that spoke the same way in store, on delivery and on social.
