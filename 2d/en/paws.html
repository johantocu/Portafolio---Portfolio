Cared for like family.

Paws of Malta looks after dogs and cats in Malta as if they were family. I designed the whole brand and took it to print, Instagram, a booking site and a digital card.

The challenge

So nobody in Malta has to choose between their life and their pet.

Create from scratch a small local brand that feels as trustworthy as family, plus a simple way to request the service: every booking is a request the team confirms in person.
